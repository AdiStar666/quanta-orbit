2. Local Game Data

Quanta Orbit stores certain game information locally on your device so that game progress and settings can be preserved.

This may include:

  • game progress and expedition progress
  • high scores and gameplay statistics
  • completed runs and game records
  • sound, volume and haptic settings
  • tutorial status

This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted by Quanta Orbit to the developer.

3. Advertising

Quanta Orbit uses Google AdMob, a mobile advertising service provided by Google.

The game may display interstitial advertisements and rewarded advertisements. Rewarded advertisements are optional advertisements that you may choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions and diagnostic information for purposes including displaying advertisements, measuring advertising performance, preventing fraud and providing advertising services.

More information about how Google handles data can be found at:

Google Privacy Policy

4. Consent and Privacy Choices

Quanta Orbit uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) where applicable to request and manage consent for advertising.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may be presented with privacy choices before advertising services are used.

Where required, privacy options can also be made available from within the game so that you can review or change your choices.

5. Internet Connection

Quanta Orbit may use an internet connection to load and display advertisements and to communicate with Google's advertising and consent services.

6. Children's Privacy

Third-party services used by the game may process data in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

7. Third-Party Services

Game progress and settings managed directly by Quanta Orbit are stored locally on your device. Third-party services are responsible for the security of information they process through their services.
